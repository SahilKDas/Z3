Z3 PHYSICS RESEARCH - Minecraft Java Edition 26.4-snapshot-3
Research date: 2026-10-09. Target: the asset repository's exact game version.

STATUS
This is a source-based port of selected default player movement rules, not
verified full Minecraft physics parity. Full parity includes every block shape,
fluid state, entity collision, pose, attribute, effect, equipment enchantment,
vehicle, damage source, input transition and server tick interaction. Those
systems are not all present in Z3. Unlimited fireworks are an intentional user
exception to survival parity. The earlier doubled jump/swim tuning is removed.

VERIFIED DEFAULT MOVEMENT RULES
Units in official code are blocks per game tick; the game tick is 1/20 second.
Z3 exposes velocity to rendering/audio in metres per second, converting by 20
only at the physics boundary. Rendering interpolates between simulation poses.

Input: horizontal input is multiplied by 0.98; item use by its movement
multiplier (ordinary eating 0.2), sneak by the default sneaking_speed 0.3.
The snapshot adjusts the input length for square movement before world rotation.
Near-zero player horizontal velocity is zeroed when its squared magnitude is
below 0.000009; vertical velocity is zeroed below magnitude 0.003.

Normal air/ground travel: accelerate, resolve movement/collision, then subtract
gravity 0.08 and apply vertical drag 0.98. Horizontal drag is block friction
multiplied by 0.91, with ordinary block friction 0.6 while grounded, and 1 in air.
Default walking movement_speed is float 0.1; sprint multiplier is 1.3.
Air acceleration is 0.02; sprint air acceleration is float 0.025999999.
For grounded friction greater than 0.6 the movement acceleration is adjusted
by 0.21600002 divided by friction cubed. The snapshot also supports friction
and air-drag modifier attributes; Z3 currently uses their default values only.

Jump: vertical impulse is float 0.42, retaining a larger existing upward
velocity. Sprint adds a horizontal impulse of 0.2 in the facing direction.
Jump exhaustion is 0.05 normally and 0.2 sprinting. Held jumping has a 10-tick
cooldown; releasing jump resets that cooldown. No coyote time or jump buffer.
The normal unobstructed jump apex is about 1.252203 blocks above the floor.
Ground friction/acceleration still apply on the initial jump tick.

Collision: standing player width 0.6 and height 1.8; crouching height 1.5;
swimming/gliding height 0.6. Resolve Y first, then the larger horizontal motion
axis, then the other horizontal axis. Z3 replaces its previous coarse movement
substeps with swept full-block AABB clipping. Sneak ledge protection reduces
horizontal movements in 0.05-block increments while checking support below.
The official step system tries candidate collision-shape heights up to default
step_height 0.6 and picks an improved horizontal result. Z3 has full cubes only;
partial shapes and the official candidate-step algorithm are still missing.

Water: jump adds 0.04 per tick, rather than assigning a fixed swim speed.
Deep water permits that impulse while on the floor. Fluid jump threshold is
0.4 normally (0 for very small eye heights). Acceleration is 0.02; horizontal
drag 0.8 ordinarily, 0.9 sprinting; vertical drag 0.8. Default non-sprint fluid
gravity subtracts 0.08/16 after drag. Swimming aims vertical movement toward
the look vector with response 0.06, or 0.085 when looking steeply down.
A swimmer can rise out of water against a bank with a 0.3 upward impulse when
horizontal collision and a free-space check permit it. It does not permit
climbing an arbitrary two-block vertical wall. Official fluid surface heights,
flow vectors, and fluid voxel intersections require a fluid-state system.
Z3's source-like full water voxels remain a simplification.

Elytra: use gravity 0.08, lift cos(pitch)^2, lift gravity multiplier 0.75,
fall-to-forward conversion 0.1, upward-look conversion 0.04 with vertical
multiplier 3.2, horizontal steering response 0.1, and drag (0.99,0.98,0.99).
No invented minimum forward speed or 55 m/s cap. Our positive-up pitch is
converted conceptually from Java's positive-down XRot convention.
Wall damage depends on horizontal speed LOST by collision: lost speed*10-3.
Wing wear is one durability per second; the last durability point disables
usable wings. Ground/water transitions end flight.

Firework entity boost occurs after player travel: add look*0.1 plus half the
difference between look*1.5 and current velocity, in blocks per tick.
Actual flight-1 rocket lifetime is 20+random(0..5)+random(0..6) ticks.
Z3 deliberately uses a repeatable 30-tick boost and unlimited launches/refills,
and refreshes an existing boost instead of stacking independent rocket entities.
That duration/stacking difference is explicitly not full rocket-entity parity.

Fall damage in this snapshot uses floor(distance+0.000001-safe_fall_distance),
with default safe distance 3, rather than blindly using an older ceil formula.
Damage modifiers, enchantments, and landing material behaviors still matter.

Hunger: exhaustion is capped at 40; only when strictly greater than 4 does
one tick spend 4 exhaustion, removing one saturation before food. Saturation
healing at full food occurs every 10 ticks: heal min(saturation,6)/6 and add
that much exhaustion. Slower healing at food >=18 occurs every 80 ticks:
heal 1 and add exhaustion 6. Starvation checks every 80 ticks; on Normal,
health does not fall below 1. Ordinary walking is not charged sprint exhaustion.
Apples: nutrition 4, saturation 2.4; cooked beef: 8 and 12.8; eating 32 ticks.

VALIDATION COMPLETED IN Z3
The regression suite includes numeric fixtures derived from the official
methods for two initial free-fall ticks, grounded acceleration and drag,
jump apex, liquid upward impulse, level-look elytra velocity, post-travel
firework velocity and fast saturation regeneration. It also checks collision,
low-food sprint gating, infinite rocket consumption behavior, equipment gating,
stack conservation, shift equipment, drag distribution, crafting and saves.
These are source-derived fixtures, not an end-to-end differential execution
of a vanilla client and server. Passing them is not proof of complete parity.

IMPLEMENTATION ORDER TO CLOSE THE GAP
First introduce block-state and collision-shape registries plus double physical
coordinates; then fluid states and exact pose transitions. Build deterministic
scenarios against a local official server: flat ground, sprint-jump course,
step/ledge/headroom obstacles, material strips, fluid edges and elytra turns.
Capture position, velocity, ground/collision/pose/fluid flags and health/food
for each tick under identical inputs. Compare traces with documented precision
tolerances; run each case across render frame rates. Only after that foundation
should effects/enchantments, entities/vehicles and survival corner cases be
added and individually checked. Full parity cannot be honestly certified by
matching a few constants or a visual smoke run.

TRIANGLE TERRAIN UPDATE
Runtime now uses continuous triangle heightfield collision instead of voxel
collision. Source-derived movement equations remain, but this terrain has no
caves, block edits, or colliding vegetation. This changes contact and stepping
behavior and must not be described as full Minecraft physics parity.

BANKED FLIGHT UPDATE
Mouse turn rate now controls automatic roll; heading follows mouse movement
only. This supersedes A/D banking, Q/C roll and bank-induced yaw.
Lift now includes cos(roll), so steep/inverted banks change descent. Camera
and avatar orientation reflect all three axes. With roll zero, the researched
base elytra equations remain unchanged. This mode is not vanilla parity.

UNRESTRICTED FLIGHT UPDATE
Runtime flight now uses an explicit unrestricted arcade model: mouse-oriented
60 m/s cruise and 102 m/s persistent boost, no collision/water cancellation,
no durability requirement or wear, full pitch loops and double mouse gain.
Diving adds speed with no hard cap. Historical source-derived vanilla equations
remain behind unrestrictedFlight=false for reference regression checks.

DO A BARREL ROLL UPDATE (supersedes runtime modes above)
Runtime unrestrictedFlight is now false. barrelFlight uses an orthonormal
local orientation: mouse X rotates around forward (roll), mouse Y around
local right (pitch); banked pitch also changes world yaw. Orientation has no
Euler angle limits. Camera and model smooth the same orthonormal basis.
The mod's default keyboard yaw is omitted to preserve requested mouse-only
flight. Vanilla equations, timed unlimited rockets, collisions, wet/ground
cancellation and durability are restored. Bank roll no longer multiplies
lift. Regression checks compare banked versus unbanked momentum at identical
look vectors and verify basis stability after 1,000 compound rotations.
Primary upstream: https://github.com/enjarai/do-a-barrel-roll

CAVE AND RAVINE UPDATE
Continuous terrain now includes seeded ravines and roofed caverns. Cavern
floors and ceilings participate in player collision; the below-world cutoff
is lowered to -2000 for this terrain. Lava contact causes burn damage. Roof
polygons are clipped to their footprint and mouth, with rim walls closing
the clipped ceiling to the rendered floor. These remain custom continuous
geometry and do not constitute Minecraft block-shape or fluid parity.
Rock below Y=0 uses deepslate; lava stays a separate emissive material.
